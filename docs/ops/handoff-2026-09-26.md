### Decisions applied (recorded in `docs/architecture.md` rule 9 and the
decisions log, `AGENTS.md`, `backlog-works-backlog` skill)

- **Id scheme:** one uppercase letter from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPRSTUVWXYZ` (I,
  O, Q excluded as digit look-alikes) plus three digits `100`-`999`, e.g.
  `K417`. Random, unique within the file, never reused, minted by
  `backlog.new_item_id` / `python3 scripts/new_id.py`. Three digits, not
  two: two digits give 2,300 ids and a 50% collision chance by ~57
  items, so a generator would need a sequential fallback anyway; three
  digits give 20,700 ids per backlog and stay two syllables to say
  ("K four-seventeen"). Alternatives considered and not taken: bare
  sequential numbers (imply an order the document already carries, and
  bare `14` is ambiguous in prose); two letters plus two digits (`KX41`,
  harder to say); four base-32 characters (`7QF3`, not memorable).
- **Views:** `Open` (default, status not Done), `Ready`, `Done`, `All`.
  No `Sprint` view (the `In Progress` rows lead the Open view in document
  order), no `Bugs` view, no count line, no filter, no card controls. An
  empty view shows "Nothing in this view.".
- **Bugs:** the `## Bugs` section, `Bug` model and `parse_bugs` are
  removed from the format, the demo file and the repo backlog. A bug is
  an ordinary row until a type field exists.
- **Repo backlog re-keyed** (old -> new): PBI-001 -> J709 (Extract
  backlog source module), PBI-002 -> U695 (Extract renderer/board),
  PBI-003 -> N835 (Standalone sign-in (magic-link email)), PBI-004 ->
  F196 (API key for agents), PBI-005 -> F122 (Railway deploy of a
